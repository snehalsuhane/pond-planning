    How are sites ranked?

    Sites favor larger connected catchments (75%) and gentler local slopes (25%). The analysis excludes mapped water and applies slope, survey-edge, and spacing checks.

    Catchments follow modeled upstream drainage, including overflow from depressions. An outlet can naturally sit at the downstream edge of its catchment.

    Annual collectible runoff = average annual rainfall × catchment area × runoff fraction. This assumes uniform rainfall and all runoff reaching the site, before storage and collection losses. Every runoff value is an estimate based on these assumptions. Specific catchment uncertainties are explained beside each affected option.
